## Camera materials

- `textures/leather/charcoal-color.webp`, `leather-normal.webp`, `leather-arm.webp`: derived from **Brown Leather**, Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven, **CC0**. https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather . Color was neutralized and converted to WebP; OpenGL normal and ARM surface maps were converted to WebP. https://polyhaven.com/license
- Material study: **Camera 01**, Rajil Jose Macatangay, Poly Haven, CC0, https://polyhaven.com/a/Camera_01 . Its separate PBR body/lens/strap and transmissive optical materials were inspected. Its geometry and UV atlas are not part of the website.
- Visual references: **Hasselblad 503 CX**, Salome_Vandereyt, CC BY 4.0, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9e8ca8a62e6b4cc4b7d8206512cd3fe1 ; **Hasselblad 5oo C/M High Poly**, Pandamboo, CC BY 4.0, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/07110878e1884aa3bb7e483f20745f32 . Their public previews were studied; no meshes or textures from these models are included.
